## Step 2: Executive Summary (Board Level)

### What is Lumio and for whom?

Lumio is an **offline-first, privacy-centric digital legacy management application** for the Dutch market. Users record personal documents, legal wishes, financial accounts, emergency contacts, medical directives, and Shamir-shared encryption keys in a fully local SQLite/SQLCipher database — no cloud, no subscription, one-time purchase at €125.

Two target audiences: **B2C individuals** (40–65yr, "bewuste voorbereider") who want their affairs in order for heirs and family in crisis moments; **B2B employers** (HR/CFO, 25–500 employees) who offer Lumio as an employee benefits tool, leveraging Dutch WKR (werkkostenregeling) exemption.

The product is technically mature for a v1.0 release on a narrow set of dimensions. It is commercially immature across multiple critical dimensions.

### Current State — Strengths

| Domain | Strength |
|--------|---------|
| **Security** | PBKDF2-SHA512 ≥310k iterations (above OWASP minimum); 5-attempt lockout; Shamir secret sharing for heir recovery; LocalOriginValidationMiddleware; AES-256 SQLCipher per-profile |

### G-GLOBAL-001 — Anti-Hallucination: No fabricated metrics
Every KPI, score, or percentage in product communication must be traceable to a verified source. Fabricated metrics in marketing copy = IMMEDIATE BLOCK.

### G-GLOBAL-002 — DEC-102: No new PostHog implementations
No new PostHog events or session recording. Existing `lumio_activated` event preserved. Violation: revert PR, escalate to PO.

### G-GLOBAL-003 — DEC-105: unsafe-inline CSP is an architectural constraint
`unsafe-inline` must NOT be removed from CSP headers until SSR migration is complete (SP-15+). No recommendation may list CSP hardening as a Phase 5 prerequisite.

### G-GLOBAL-004 — DEC-103: Squash merge strategy
Maximum 1 active feature branch alongside `main`. PRs from feature branches only. Violation: no merge until prior feature branch is squash-merged.

### G-GLOBAL-005 — Legal disclaimer gate
Stories touching euthanasia, organ donation, or testament modules must carry a `LEGAL_REVIEW: REQUIRED` annotation and receive written PO sign-off before merge. Source: COMPLIANCE_RISK-003.

### G-GLOBAL-006 — Privacy policy prerequisite
No feature enabling data processing beyond current state may be deployed until REC-LEGAL-001 (privacy policy) is live. Source: GAP-LEGAL-001.

### G-GLOBAL-007 — B2B gate: legal framework first
No B2B customer may be onboarded until joint-controller agreement template (GAP-LEGAL-002) and privacy policy are complete. Source: SYSTEM_RISK-P2-002.

### G-BIZ-001 — Revenue model before marketing investment
No paid channel spend or content marketing investment may be committed until a revenue model is documented (SP-BIZ-01-003) and payment provider decided (BLK-001). Source: REC-BIZ-001.

### G-TECH-001 — CI gate
No story may be marked `Done` while CI is disabled for its test suite. BLOCKED stories may be written; implementation is gated on CI restoration. Source: SYS-RISK-002.

### G-TECH-002 — Video encryption prerequisite
REC-DATA-001 (video encryption) must NOT be scheduled until Q-09-001 (video file sizes) is answered. Source: PLANNING_RISK-P2-001.
