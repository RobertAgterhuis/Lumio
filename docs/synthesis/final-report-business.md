## 1. Summary for This Team

Lumio is a commercially promising product built on a technically sound foundation, but it has not yet defined the commercial scaffold needed to generate or scale revenue. The core product — offline-first, privacy-centric digital legacy management — fills a real market gap in the Netherlands and aligns with demographic trends (ageing population, increasing financial complexity for mid-career professionals). However, three structural gaps prevent revenue: no automated payment flow, no formal pricing/revenue model documentation, and no B2B commercial framework.

The most urgent business risks are not strategic — they are legal. Three content modules (euthanasia directive, organ donation, testament) are live without the obligatory legal disclaimers that distinguish Lumio's document capture from legally certified declarations. This constitutes an active liability that affects every current and future user. These cannot be deferred to a later sprint. Additionally, no user-facing privacy policy exists, which is a material AVG art. 13 violation for a product that processes special-category health data.

The good news: all three LAUNCH BLOCKERs are cheap to fix (3–6 days combined), and the product's privacy-first architecture and WKR-eligible B2B channel represent genuine competitive moats that competitors would struggle to replicate quickly. The strategic path is clear — stabilise legal compliance first, then fund and accelerate commercial development.
