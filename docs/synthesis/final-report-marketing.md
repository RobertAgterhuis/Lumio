## 1. Summary for This Team

Lumio's marketing position has a compelling story to tell — offline-first, privacy-centric, one-time payment, designed for Dutch life-planning culture — but the delivery infrastructure is almost entirely absent. The most critical finding: every B2C visitor who intends to purchase must send an email (`mailto:info@lumio.app?subject=Lumio kopen`) and wait for a manual response. There is no automated B2C revenue path. This single gap makes all B2C marketing investment (paid channels, SEO, CRO experiments) commercially inert.

Three marketing-specific quick wins are available at LOW effort and should be completed in Sprint 1 regardless of the checkout decision: (1) fix the B2B-framed OG metaTitle (`layout.tsx` L26 — 1 line change); (2) add Plausible custom goals and fix the blind EXP-003 A/B experiment (both fire DOM events to nowhere); (3) add JSON-LD structured data (Organisation + WebPage) to all pages (estimated 0.5 day). These three changes collectively take ~2 days and directly improve SEO CTR, enable data-informed decisions, and eliminate legal risk from the active blind A/B test.

The domain `lumio-legacy.nl` is the largest brand risk outside the checkout gap. "Legacy" carries death/inheritance associations for Dutch consumers, directly undermining the core B2C message of "peace of mind and preparedness." A brand-level domain decision should be made in Q1.

The testimonial situation requires immediate resolution: three hardcoded names ("Marieke van den Berg", "Thomas Kleijn", "Pieter Hoogenbosch") in `TestimonialsSection.tsx` have no documented consent or origin. If fabricated or used without consent, this creates exposure under _Wet Misleidende Handelspraktijken_. The cheapest fix is a "representatief citaat" disclaimer.

- **G-MKT-001 (testimonials):** All testimonials must have documented consent or carry "representatief citaat" disclaimer before merge. No fabricated or unattributed quotes. Source: RISK-MKT-003.

- **G-MKT-003 (brand tone):** B2B copy uses formal "u/uw". B2C copy uses informal "je/jij". Mixed-audience contexts require PO sign-off. Source: BRAND_CONSISTENCY-001.  
- **G-BIZ-001 (revenue model gate):** No paid channel spend until revenue model (SP-BIZ-01-003) and payment provider (BLK-001) are decided. Source: phase4-analysis.md.  
- **G-GLOBAL-002 (DEC-102):** No new PostHog implementations. Analytics work is Plausible-only. Existing `lumio_activated` event permitted.  
- **G-GLOBAL-001 (anti-hallucination):** No fabricated metrics in copy or marketing materials. All claims must be traceable to a verified source. CBS assumptions in RoiCalculator must be labelled as estimates until verified.
