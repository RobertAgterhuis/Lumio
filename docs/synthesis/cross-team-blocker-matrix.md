## Reading Guide

**Rows** = team that is blocked or needs input.  
**Columns** = team that must deliver or decide.  
Cell = description of the dependency + classification (`BLOCKING` / `ADVISORY`).

A `BLOCKING` dependency means the requesting team **cannot start or complete** the relevant item without it.  
An `ADVISORY` dependency means the other team can help or provide input but is not a hard prerequisite.
