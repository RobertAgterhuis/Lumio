# 15 — Video Messages

The **Video Messages** page lets you record or upload personal video messages for your heirs. Each message can be addressed to one or more specific recipients and will be accessible to them when they unlock the application after your passing.

Navigate to Video Messages via the **sidebar** under the "Tools" group, or by pressing `G` then `V` on the keyboard.

---

## Adding a Video Message

Click **New message** at the top right of the page to open the video dialog.

### Step 1 — Choose how to add a video

The dialog offers two options:

| Tab | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Record** | Record a video directly from your webcam. A maximum recording time applies (default: 5 minutes). |
| **Upload** | Select an existing video file from your computer. |

For the **Record** tab, click **Start recording** to begin. Click **Stop recording** when done. You can preview the recording before saving.

For the **Upload** tab, click the upload button and select a video file from your device. The file size and duration are shown after selection.

### Step 2 — Fill in the details

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Title** | A short, recognizable name for the video (required) |
| **Description** | Optional additional notes |
| **Recipients** | Select one or more heirs from your heirs list |

> **Tip**: You can leave the recipients list empty if you want the message to be accessible to any heir who unlocks the application.

### Step 3 — Save

Click **Save** to upload and store the video. A progress bar shows the upload status. Video data is stored encrypted in the Lumio database — it never leaves your device.

---

## Viewing Your Video Messages

Each saved video message appears as a card on the page, showing:

- **Title** and description
- **Recipients** — which heirs the message is addressed to
- **File size** and duration
- **Date** the message was added

### Playing a Video

Click the **Play** button (▶) on any card to open the built-in video player. The video plays directly within the application.

---

## Editing a Video Message

Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) to change the title, description, or recipients of an existing message. You cannot replace the video file itself — to change the video you must delete the message and create a new one.

---

## Deleting a Video Message

Click the **Delete** button (trash icon) on the card. A confirmation dialog will appear asking you to confirm deletion. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

---

## Storage Limit

The page header shows how many messages you have used out of the permitted maximum (e.g., "3 of 10 messages used"). Once the limit is reached, the **New message** button is disabled. Delete an existing message to free up space.

---

## Section Notes

You can add a private note to this section using the notes field at the top of the page. This note is visible only to you and is not included in exports.

---

## Availability in Heir Mode

When heirs unlock the application using emergency codes, they can view and play all video messages. Messages can only be played — heirs cannot add, edit, or delete messages in heir mode.
