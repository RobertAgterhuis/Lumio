### 2.2 Data Processing Activities

| Activity | Legal Basis | Special Category | DPIA Required | DPIA Status |
|----------|------------|-----------------|--------------|-------------|
| Store owner personal data + BSN | Contractual necessity (art. 6(1)(b)) | No | No | N/A |
| Store euthanasia directive | Explicit consent (art. 9(2)(a)) | Yes (health) | Yes | ✅ v1.0 approved |
| Store donor registration | Explicit consent (art. 9(2)(a)) | Yes (health) | Yes | ✅ v1.0 approved |
| Store heir personal data + BSN | Contractual necessity (art. 6(1)(b)) | No | No | N/A |
| Heir BSN processing | INSUFFICIENT_DATA: legal basis not separately stated in DPIA | No (but sensitive) | No | ⚠️ DPIA amendment needed |
| PostHog analytics (`lumio_activated` event — **alleen Lumio-desktop app**, niet marketingsite; DEC-111) | INSUFFICIENT_DATA: consent vs. legitimate interest (Q-33-001) | No | No | DPA needed |
| Azure Application Insights (marketingsite `lumio-legacy.nl` — DEC-113, **gepland SP-3-003**) | AVG art. 6(1)(f) legitiem belang (cookievrij, alleen pageview/conversie, zero-PII; DEC-114) | No | No | ✅ Azure DPA

### 2.3 Retention Schedule Summary

| Data Category | Retention Period | Legal Basis | Technical Enforcement |
|--------------|-----------------|------------|----------------------|
| Owner + all associated data | Until account deletion | Contractual necessity | Cascade delete |
| Special category data (health) | Until account deletion or consent withdrawal | Art. 9(2)(a) consent | Cascade delete |
| BSN | Same as owner | Legitimate interest (legal docs) | Cascade delete |
| Audit log | 90 days | Security logging obligation | Background rotation service |
| Video messages | Until account deletion | Contractual necessity | Cascade delete + file cleanup |
